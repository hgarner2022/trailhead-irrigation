# Data model: every field, where it lives, who sets it, where it shows

**The rule: a change to a field is not done until every cell of its row is updated, and every PR that adds a field adds its row.** A field that shows in the checkout but not in the admin, or in the table but not the panel, is the bug the owner found on 13 Nov 2026 ("Mid May" in the Passes table beside a panel that knew "Apr 26"; a customer with a first name and no last name). The visit row is the only source for a day; screens read it and nothing derives it twice.

## Two words, one meaning each

**A job is the sold work**: a Season Pass, or a repair, an installation or other work booked by the office or the site. It has a customer, an address, a price, a status (pending, active, done, cancelled) and a source (checkout, office, site). **A visit is one stop on a job**: a day, a time, a person (the truck is a label on them), minutes, a state, notes and a charge. A pass is a job of kind pass, with two or three visits. The admin says "visit" for the stop and "job" only for the pass or the booked work.

Written 13 Nov 2026 from `src/db/schema.ts`, `src/lib/admin/{types,shape,jobs}.ts` and the screens. Docs means `public/llms.txt`, `public/pricing.md` and `docs/*`; a dash means the field is not shown there on purpose.

## The two pills on every visit

Every visit row and panel shows two pills, derived by `statesOf` in `src/lib/admin/jobs.ts` from the one row. **Work**: No day yet (no day), Scheduled (has a day), Done, Not done (with the reason, while it has no day again), Cancelled. A Scheduled visit whose day has passed stays Scheduled with a small "day passed" note, never a state; Routed is a start time in the Time column, not a state. **Money**: nothing while the work is not Done (the pill is not drawn) or while the visit is cancelled or skipped with nothing paid, then Paid (with how: "Paid, card", "Paid, check", "Paid, Venmo", "Paid, cash"; only once the visit's rows net to its whole price, one rule, `isPaid`, read by the pill, the state, the pass panel and `paymentStatus`; a visit paid and then cancelled or skipped keeps showing its Paid pill while the money is held), Owed (a part-paid visit, $100 of $145, is Owed, with the number on a job: "$115 owed"), Bill sent, Failed (the visit's newest payment row did not go through). Every one of them is derived by `src/lib/money/words.ts` (`visitMoney`, `jobMoney`) from the payment rows (`charge`, refunds as negative rows) and never from `visit.charged_at` or a typed flag; Owed is the one word for money still to come. A job (a pass or booked work) carries the same money across its visits: "Paid", "$115 owed" or "1 of 2 paid, $115 owed" (`jobMoney`, from the job's lines and its payment rows). Bill sent is read from the invoice (step 4 task 4.3): a done job that still owes money and has an open bill (`invoice` not paid, not cancelled) reads Bill sent on the visits its frozen lines name (every visit of the job when the lines name none) and on the job (`jobMoney`'s `billSent`); an overdue bill still reads Bill sent (and has its own row on Home, "Bill overdue", with Remind), a cancelled or paid one does not. A part-paid visit is never counted as paid in a job's "1 of 2 paid" (`jobMoney` reads `isPaid` against each visit's price). The older single-state vocabulary below still drives the counts and the customer's pass page, and takes its Charged and Charge failed from the same money words.

## Visit states: the older single vocabulary

`src/lib/admin/jobs.ts` derives it in one function from the row, and every screen uses it. No schema enum was added: the words map onto the existing rows.

| Admin word | Read from |
|---|---|
| No day yet | `visit.status` unscheduled, no `scheduled_on` |
| Scheduled | `scheduled_on` set, `start_time` empty |
| Routed | `start_time` set |
| Done | `completed_on` set (status completed), no succeeded payment row on the visit and no share of a payment made on the job as a whole (money word Owed) |
| Charged | the visit's payment rows, plus its share of any payment made on the job as a whole (`allocateJobPayments`: a bill paid on the pay page), net to its whole price (`isPaid`; money word Paid, with how, "Paid, card" for the bill); the old `charged_at` flag is not read |
| Charge failed | the visit's newest payment row failed, or the visit is done and `customer.card_failed_at` is set (money word Failed) |
| Cancelled, Skipped | `visit.status` cancelled, skipped |
| Not closed (a badge, not a state) | a day that has passed on a visit not marked done |

A pending pass is a checkout not finished (expires after an hour). Its visits are never on the Schedule, in the No day yet list or in Home's counts, and its status reads Pending, never Active.

**The two Home controls for work and money** (step 4 task 4.5; both are worked out from the rows, never stored). A visit whose day has passed with no Done sits on Home until it is marked Done or moved (not closed). A job that is Done and Owed sits on Home until a payment row exists (owed), or until a bill is sent, after which its invoice sits there once it is overdue, with Remind. The three rows are exclusive for one job: owed has no row for a job with an open bill, and the overdue row goes when the bill is paid or cancelled (cancelled, the owed row returns). Nothing sends a reminder on its own: Remind is a button, and two presses in a day send one message. The Visits header's "N done and owed" and Home's "Owed on N done jobs" count the same thing, Done visits whose money word is Owed, a job once (`owedRows`, `jobCount` in `visits-model.ts`); Bill sent and Failed are different words and are not in that count.

## A pass sold by the office

`passes.sell` (scope write; New pass on Visits, Sell a pass on the customer panel; `docs/plans/2026-11-16-office-sells-a-pass.md`) makes in one call exactly the rows a website sale makes, through the same code (`makePassRows`, `markPassActive`, `finishPurchase`): the job (kind pass, source office), the pass active from the start, its two or three visits with the days typed (each placed like a move), the consent with source admin, the `pass-onboard` task and the purchase confirmation. Price is never typed; it comes from the zones through `src/lib/season-pass.ts`. The one thing it may lack is the card: an active pass with no card is paid at each visit (Done asks how, like a one-off, and no charge task runs) until a card link turns it into a card pass.
