# UX audit: the customer's side (Nov 18 2026)

Read as a Trailhead customer on a phone. Nothing in code was changed, nothing was sent, no confirm button was pressed. Live pages were read in the signed-in Chrome group as the customer "Hannah Garner" (two Repair visits on Nov 18, one canceled, one paid, no card, no pass, nothing upcoming). Texts and emails were read by running the real renderers (`renderEmail`, `SMS_TEMPLATES`) on the fixtures in `src/emails/fixtures.ts`. Section 5 says what could not be tested.

Method note for the phone pass: `resize_window` to 390x844 did not take (the tab stayed 1703 px wide). Every live page was therefore loaded in a same-origin 390 px iframe, so layout, media queries, tap-target sizes, scroll width and input font sizes are real; iOS Safari behaviour (zoom, keyboard, safe areas) is inferred from them, not observed.

Severity: **blocks** = a customer cannot finish or is misled into a dead end; **confuses** = a customer hesitates, misreads or has to ask Ryan; **polish** = a small miss against the pattern.

## 2. Things that don't make sense

1. The day-before text and email invite a change the account then refuses (row 1). The text, the email and the page disagree about whether the customer may act.
2. "Another day? Doesn't work? Pick another day:" in one text (row 4).
3. The Reschedule sheet, checkout and every message sell a day with a window later, while the settled product is day plus exact start time (row 11).
4. A signed-in customer sees "Sign in" in the marketing header and the sign-in form on `/account/sign-in` (rows 10, 17).
5. A $145 pay page says it keeps the card for later visits (row 6).
6. Two Nov 18 Repair rows, one canceled and one paid, sit next to each other with no time, so the list cannot be read (row 8). (This is Hannah's test data, but any customer with a same-day rebook will see it.)
7. The receipt email has a receipt number; the receipt page in the account does not (row 14).
8. The receipt email says "Your fall blowout is planned for early October." to a repair customer who may have no blowout; it reads as a promise (a pass customer's receipt only; check it never reaches one-offs).
9. "Someone must be home for our visits" is saved but changes nothing in scheduling (row 18).
10. The account has no entry for a canceled visit's next step, while the cancel email says to text Ryan (row 9).

## 3. Research (what the field does, and the call for us)

Searches were run Nov 18 2026; where a source did not say, that is stated rather than guessed. Several vendor help centers did not load their own text, so the evidence is the search summaries plus the named official pages.

**Jobber client hub.** Jobber describes the hub as a self-serve place to approve quotes, check appointment details and see the assigned team; its listed features do not name a client-side reschedule or cancel. Jobber's January 2026 AI Receptionist matches a caller to the client record and can reschedule or cancel by call or text. When a business reschedules a visit Jobber prompts to send a notification. Sources: [what do your clients see in client hub](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub/), [customer self service portal examples](https://www.getjobber.com/academy/customer-self-service-portal-examples/), [how to prevent customer cancellations](https://www.getjobber.com/academy/how-to-prevent-customer-cancellations/). Call: our self-serve reschedule and cancel go further than the hub's documented features. Keep them. Follow Jobber's "prompt to notify on reschedule" (we already text on every move).

**Housecall Pro.** The "On my way" text goes only by text (never email), carries the customer's first name, company, technician first and last name, an optional technician photo and, if added, the service address; replies go to the Customer Inbox. Sources: [customer notifications overview](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/357613-customer-notifications-overview), [customize sms notifications](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12417565-customize-sms-notifications), [service address now included in appointment texts](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/16543183-service-address-now-included-in-appointment-texts). No official page promised a live ETA. Call: our on-my-way text already matches (text only, first name, street, link). Do not promise tracking. A photo is not worth building for one truck.

**ServiceTitan.** Customers confirm or change by replying to the text; the booking confirmation goes out only once and a reschedule does not send another; arrival windows in texts and emails can be the business-hours slot, a start time only, or a start time plus job duration. Sources: [Enable booking confirmations](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/enable-booking-confirmations), [Set up arrival windows](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/set-up-arrival-windows-for-customer-notifications-and-confirmations), [confirming appointments](https://www.servicetitan.com/guides/contractor-playbook/confirming-appointments). Call: do not copy "reply Confirmed" (we use YES only for a must-be-home property); do keep re-sending on a move (they do not, customers get lost). Start-time-plus-duration is the model for Hannah's exact-time decision.

**Square Appointments.** Clients reach self-service from the confirmation email or text ("Reschedule or Cancel"), the cancellation policy shows in the booking flow and at the foot of confirmation emails, a client who tries outside the window is reminded of the policy, and Square advises sending reminders early enough to act before the cutoff. Sources: [cancellation policy](https://squareup.com/us/en/appointments/features/cancellation-policy), [How to Protect your Services and Time](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Square-Appointments/How-to-Protect-your-Services-and-Time-with-Square-Appointments/m-p/633308). Call: this is the pattern we follow most closely, and it exposes row 1: our day-before reminder arrives after the cutoff and still offers a change. Follow Square on three points: the action link goes to that appointment (row 7), the policy line sits with the action ("Changes online until Monday 11:59 pm"), and the reminder before the cutoff is the two-week text.

**Calendly.** Cancel and reschedule links are an opt-in per event type and appear together; once canceled, reschedule disappears. The host's cancel dialog has an optional message; no source showed an invitee-side reason field. Sources: [How to include cancel and reschedule links](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/28926585452951), [How to add a cancellation policy](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/28926500961431). Call: our optional "Tell Ryan why" is more than Calendly asks and is fine. Follow Calendly's rule that a canceled booking should still offer a way to book again (row 9).

**NN/g and Baymard.** The NN/g transactional-email report is paywalled; only its scope (order and service confirmations, notifications) could be confirmed, so no NN/g specifics are cited. Baymard's mobile receipt benchmark says a confirmation page cannot cause abandonment but users hesitate to leave until the email arrives, and its confirmation-email guideline lists what to include (order number, payment, delivery details). Baymard's mobile keyboard article asks for purpose-built keyboards and the right input type, and labels above fields. Sources: [receipt benchmark](https://baymard.com/mcommerce-usability/benchmark/mobile-page-types/receipt), [guideline 597](https://baymard.com/guidelines/597-what-information-to-include-in-the-confirmation-email), [mobile touch keyboards](https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-touch-keyboards). Baymard's own pages did not state a 44 px figure; 44 pt comes from Apple's guidelines and 48 dp from Material, both quoted by secondary sources, and the 16 px rule (iOS zooms into a focused field under 16 px) is common platform knowledge that was not re-sourced here. Call: follow Baymard on receipt number (row 14), on keyboards (already correct on sign-in and zones), and on a confirmation page that gives the receipt immediately (row 13).

## 4. What is good and should not change

1. Every page measured at 390 px has no horizontal scroll (scroll width 390 on Home, Visits, visit, receipt, details, pass, sign-in, the pay not-found, `/book` and the checkout step).
2. The visit page and timeline use plain words and one status pill (Booked, Done, Canceled, Pick a day); never job, line, stage or request.
3. "Keep my visit" as the ghost way back, "None of these? Text Ryan", and the one sentence that says why changes this close go through Ryan (from the source of `CancelVisitSheet`, `RescheduleSheet` and `TextRyan`; the sheets themselves could not be opened, see section 5).
4. The 24-hour rule is decided once in the API, so a button and its refusal agree, and the same rule drives Text Ryan / Call Ryan.
5. Fact-first subjects and leads in every email ("Receipt: $178 for your repair at 480 Briggs St", "Canceled: your spring turn-on at 480 Briggs St"), one button, "The Trailhead crew", the same footer on all of them.
6. The sign-in email says where the link goes (host), that it works once and for 10 minutes, and what to do if you did not ask; the `/sign-in/confirm` step ("This link is not valid... Get a new link") protects against mail scanners that burn a one-time link.
7. Field attributes on Details and sign-in: `autocomplete` is `given-name`, `family-name`, `tel`, `email`; the sign-in field is `type=email`; zones is `inputmode=numeric`.
8. The checkout address box is 18 px, a combobox with autocomplete off, with a typed-address fallback always one tap away; the checkout reads "We work in Erie, Longmont, Louisville, Lafayette, Firestone and Broomfield" before the customer wastes a step.
9. A cancel email says "Nothing was charged." and the texts end "Reply STOP to opt out." only on a number's first text.
10. A customer with no pass sees "No Season Pass on this account yet" with one next step, not an empty page.

## 5. What could not be tested, and why

1. **The Reschedule and Cancel sheets, and the 24-hour-window states.** Hannah's record has no upcoming visit (both are done or canceled), so the controls do not render, and confirming either would change live data. They were read from `RescheduleSheet.tsx`, `CancelVisitSheet.tsx`, `TextRyan.tsx` and `VisitControls.tsx` only. Needs a seeded upcoming visit (or Storybook at 375).
2. **Storybook stories at 375.** Not run (the worktree has no `node_modules` and the run budget was spent on live pages and the renderers). Emails were read as plain text from the real renderers, not as rendered HTML on a phone, so the email layout, button size, preheader and dark-mode rendering were not seen.
3. **A real phone.** `resize_window` did not resize the Chrome window, so the phone pass used a 390 px same-origin iframe. Not observed: iOS Safari field zoom, the keyboard covering inputs, safe-area insets, back-button behaviour in a sheet, tap accuracy, system text size, the 320 px width.
4. **The signed one-visit link page.** It needs a signed token for an upcoming visit; none was available and none was created (creating one means sending a text). Expired, tampered and signed-out behaviour of `?t=` is untested.
5. **The signed-out sign-in flow.** Pressing "Email me a link" sends a real email, so only the pages were read.
6. **`/account/pass` with a pass, the pay page with an open invoice, and checkout steps 2 to 7.** No pass or open invoice exists on this record, and the checkout was stopped at step 1 as instructed. Their words were read from source (`PayScreen.tsx`, `checkout-*.tsx`) and the strings quoted in the table.
7. **Texts as received.** No text was sent; lengths and wording come from `SMS_TEMPLATES` on fixture data, whose window and `asked` flags may differ from production.
8. **Live Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Square and Calendly screens.** Only their public help pages and search summaries were read; no product was used, and several help pages did not return their own text.
