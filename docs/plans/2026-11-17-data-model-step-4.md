# Data model step 4 implementation plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (or subagent-driven-development in this session) to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Money is a record, not a flag. Every dollar that arrives is a payment row on the job (a refund is a negative row pointing at the payment it reverses); a job is Paid when its succeeded payments equal its total and Owed with the number otherwise; "Send a bill" writes an invoice with its lines frozen, sends one message with a Stripe-hosted pay page, and an overdue invoice sits on Home with Remind.

**Architecture:** The `charge` table keeps its name and becomes the payment record (`job_id`, nullable `visit_id`, `paid_on`, `refunds_payment_id`); the pure money words live in `src/lib/money/words.ts` and every screen reads them from the payment rows. An `invoice` table holds the frozen lines, amount, tax, sent, due and paid dates. The pay page is a public token page that opens a Stripe Checkout Session in payment mode; the webhook writes the payment row and marks the invoice paid. `visit.charged_at` and `visit.stripe_payment_intent_id` keep being written as aliases until step 6. Sales tax is in the model and off by default: an organization setting turns it on when Ryan answers.

**Tech Stack:** as step 3 (`docs/plans/2026-11-17-data-model-step-3.md` header), plus Stripe Checkout Sessions in payment mode (`stripe.checkout.sessions.create({ mode: "payment" })`) through the existing `getStripe()` and `stubStripe` test double.

Design: `docs/plans/2026-11-16-data-model-design.md`, sections "Lifecycles: read from facts, never typed", "Collecting payment", "Open question for Ryan". Then `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` (the money rows: Done, Charged, Charge failed, `charge.*`, `visit.charged_at`, `visit.amount`), `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/UX_WRITING.md`, and `src/lib/admin/jobs.ts` (the two pills and `statesOf`).

**Rules that hold for every task** are the step 3 header's (a push migrates production, so the branch `step-4-payments-invoice` is pushed only when the step is done; backfills idempotent; seeds safe on real rows; old columns written as aliases; every field in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` in the same commit; commit trailer; tsc and unit before every commit; sentence case, no em dashes). Money on screen uses the design's words: **Paid** (with how: "Paid, card", "Paid, check", "Paid, Venmo", "Paid, cash"), **Owed** (with the number on a job: "$115 owed"), **Bill sent**, **Failed**. "Unpaid" goes away.

**Decisions made here (Hannah can reverse any of them)**

- Nothing is ever deleted: Done's undo is allowed only while the job has no payment row; after that the office refunds the payment and the visit stays Done. The message says "A payment was recorded. Refund it from the job first."
- A refund of a card payment goes through Stripe (`stripe.refunds.create`); a refund of check, Venmo or cash is a record only. Both are a negative payment row with `refunds_payment_id`.
- Sales tax: `settings.billing.collectsSalesTax` (default false) and `settings.billing.salesTaxPercent` (default 0). When off, no tax line anywhere. When on, an invoice's tax is the taxable subtotal times the percent, rounded to the dollar; the checkout stays untouched this step.
- Payment term: `settings.billing.dueDays` (default 14). The bill is due that many days after it is sent.
- Reminders are a button (Remind on Home and on the invoice), never automatic. Each press sends one message with a key per day, so two presses in a day send one.
- The pay page keeps the card: the Checkout Session sets `payment_intent_data.setup_future_usage: "off_session"` and the webhook saves the card on the customer (step 3's `cardColumns`). The page says so in one line above the button: "We keep the card for the visits after this one."
