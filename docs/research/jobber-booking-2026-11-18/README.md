# Jobber's online booking, as a Trailhead customer sees it

Walked on Nov 18 2026 at https://www.trailheadirrigation.com/book (the Jobber form embedded there), in a browser, with test details, not submitted. These screenshots are the reference for how a customer books with Jobber today and the bar our own booking flow must meet. Hannah's rule, Nov 18 2026: the customer picks an exact time, as here.

| Step | Screenshot | What the customer does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-zip-code.jpg` | Enters a zip code to start ("Enter your zip code to get started"). |
| 2 | `02-services-with-durations.jpg`, `03-services-list.jpg` | Ticks one or more services; each shows its price line and its length (30m, 1h, 1h 30m, 2h). |
| 3 | `04-contact.jpg`, `05-address.jpg` | First and last name, email (marketing opt-in ticked by default), phone with the SMS consent line, street address with Google suggestions, unit, city, state, zip. |
| 4 | `06-service-details.jpg` | Service details: "I confirm that access will be available to the sprinkler shut-off valve and controller" (required), number of zones, a notes box, photo upload, "How did you hear about us?". |
| 5 | `07-date-and-time-slots.jpg` | **Schedule an appointment**: a month calendar on the left (days with no availability greyed), and on the right the open slots for the chosen day, each the service's length ("12:00 PM – 12:30 PM", "12:30 PM – 1:00 PM", "4:00 PM – 4:30 PM", "4:30 PM – 5:00 PM") with "Estimated duration: 30m" and the time zone. The customer picks one. |
| 6 | not captured | Review and confirm. |

What to take: the exact-slot step (5), the service length shown up front (2), the access confirmation (4). What to do better: fewer steps (our address type-ahead and contact can be one screen), slots ordered by what fits Ryan's route with the best preselected, and the Season Pass as one booking with two slots.
