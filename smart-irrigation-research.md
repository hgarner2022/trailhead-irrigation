## 2. MPR (Matched Precipitation Rate) Nozzles

### What Are They?
Matched Precipitation Rate (MPR) means all sprinkler heads in a zone apply water at the **same rate regardless of arc or radius**, ensuring uniform coverage. Rotary nozzles (like MP Rotators) deliver water in **slow, rotating multi-stream patterns** rather than traditional fixed spray fans.

### Water Savings
- **30-50% more efficient** than standard fixed spray nozzles
- Independent water audits show **up to 30% water savings** when retrofitting existing systems with MP Rotators
- Apply water at **~0.4 in/hour** vs ~1.5 in/hour for standard sprays (roughly 1/4 the rate)
- Slower application rate **prevents runoff** on slopes and clay soils
- Better uniformity = less overwatering of some areas to compensate for dry spots

### Pricing Per Nozzle (Retail)
| Brand/Model | Retail Price (each) | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| Hunter MP Rotator (MP1000, MP2000, MP3000) | $5-$7 each | ~$7 each on Amazon in 10-packs |
| Rain Bird R-VAN (R-VAN14, R-VAN18, R-VAN24) | $6.49-$6.99 each | From Rain Bird store; sold in 10-packs |

### Key Brands & Models
- **Hunter MP Rotator** -- most popular; models MP1000 (8-15 ft), MP2000 (13-21 ft), MP3000 (22-30 ft), MP3500 (31-35 ft). Best at 35-40 PSI.
- **Rain Bird R-VAN** -- competitive alternative; models R-VAN14 (8-14 ft), R-VAN18 (13-18 ft), R-VAN24 (17-24 ft). Best at 30 PSI or lower.
- Both have adjustable arcs and matched precipitation rates
- Hunter MP has longer throw distance; Rain Bird R-VAN has wider arc range

### Cost to Retrofit a Zone
- Nozzle-only swap: $5-$7 per head x typical 8-12 heads per zone = **$40-$84 in nozzles per zone**
- If bodies also need replacing (Hunter PROS-04 spray bodies): add ~$5-$8 per head
- **Total materials per zone: $80-$180**
- **Professional labor to retrofit a zone: $100-$200** (1-2 hours)

## 3. Drip Irrigation Zones

### Water Savings vs Spray Heads
- **30-70% less water** than conventional spray sprinklers (for shrubs, trees, garden beds)
- Common cited range: **40-60% water reduction**
- Drip system efficiency: **~90%** (water reaching root zone)
- Spray head efficiency: **~60-75%**
- Savings come from: eliminating wind drift, evaporation, and runoff

### Components Needed for a Drip Zone Conversion
| Component | Purpose | Approximate Cost |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| Pressure regulator/reducer | Reduces to 15-30 PSI for drip | $10-$25 |
| Filter (mesh/disc) | Prevents emitter clogging | $8-$20 |
| Combo filter/regulator unit | 2-in-1 space saver | $15-$30 |
| 1/2" mainline drip tubing | Distribution backbone (per 100 ft) | $15-$30 |
| 1/4" micro tubing | Branch lines to individual plants | $5-$10 per 50 ft |
| Emitters/drippers | Point-source watering (per unit) | $0.25-$1.00 each |
| Drip line (inline emitters) | Pre-spaced emitters in tubing | $20-$40 per 100 ft |
| Fittings, connectors, stakes | Assembly hardware | $10-$25 |

### Cost Range for Materials (Per Zone)
- **DIY materials only:** $50-$200 per zone depending on size and complexity
- **Professional conversion (materials + labor):** $300-$1,200 per zone
- **Average residential drip install:** $310-$815 (HomeAdvisor)
- **Cost per square foot:** $0.50-$5.00

### Best Applications
- Flower beds, shrub borders, garden beds
- Tree rings and foundation plantings
- Slopes where spray would cause runoff
- NOT recommended for large turf areas (spray/rotor heads still better for lawns)

- Front Range outdoor water use: **~55% of residential water** in urban areas (CSU Extension data), most on turf

- A 30% reduction in irrigation = **4,500-6,000 fewer gallons/month**

- Over a 5-month irrigation season: **$275-$545 annual savings** from smart controller alone
- Combined upgrades (smart controller + MPR nozzles + drip conversion): potential **$400-$800+ annual savings**
