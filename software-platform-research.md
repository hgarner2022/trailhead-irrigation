## 2. GENERAL PAYMENT / SUBSCRIPTION BILLING PLATFORMS

These are payment-first platforms. They handle billing well but lack field service features (scheduling, route optimization, etc.).

---

### Stripe
**Website:** stripe.com

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Monthly Cost** | $0 (no monthly fee, no setup fee, no cancellation fee) |
| **Transaction Fees** | 2.9% + $0.30 per card transaction. ACH: 0.8% (capped at $5). International cards: +1.5%. Stripe Billing adds 0.7% on subscription volume. Disputed charges: $15. |
| **QuickBooks Integration** | No native integration. Requires third-party tools: Synder (~$50/mo), Acodei, PayTraQer, or Zapier. |
| **Recurring Billing** | Yes. Excellent. Stripe Billing handles prorations, metered billing, dunning (failed payment retry), subscription management, customer portal. |
| **CRM** | No. Payment data only. |
| **Scheduling** | No. |
| **Mobile App** | Stripe Dashboard app (view-only for monitoring, no field features). |

**Pros:**
- No monthly fees (pay only when you process)
- Best-in-class subscription billing engine
- ACH payments at 0.8% capped at $5 -- significant savings for recurring payments
- Developer-friendly, extremely reliable
- Customer portal for managing subscriptions
- Volume discounts available at $100K+/mo

**Cons:**
- No CRM, scheduling, or field service features
- QBO integration requires paid third-party tool ($50+/mo)
- 0.7% additional fee for Stripe Billing on subscription volume
- Technical to set up without a developer
- Not designed for service businesses

**Effective cost on a $150/mo lawn subscription (card):**
- 2.9% + $0.30 = $4.65
- Plus 0.7% Billing fee = $1.05
- Total per transaction: $5.70 (3.8% effective rate)

**With ACH instead:**
- 0.8% = $1.20
- Plus 0.7% Billing fee = $1.05
- Total per transaction: $2.25 (1.5% effective rate)

## 3. STANDALONE CRM OPTIONS

**Important context:** Most lawn care businesses at the 25-100 customer scale do NOT need a standalone CRM. The CRM built into Jobber, GorillaDesk, or similar field service tools is sufficient. A standalone CRM makes sense only if you are NOT using an all-in-one field service platform.

---

## 4. QUICKBOOKS ONLINE NATIVE CAPABILITIES

### Can QBO Handle Recurring Subscriptions on Its Own?

**Yes, with limitations.**

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Recurring Invoices** | Available on all plans. Create templates that auto-send on a schedule (weekly, monthly, quarterly, etc.). |

| **Customer Management** | Basic. Customer list with contact info, transaction history, notes. Not a real CRM. |
| **Scheduling** | No. |
| **Route Optimization** | No. |
| **Mobile App** | Yes, but for accounting/invoicing only. No field service features. |

### QBO Plan Pricing (March 2026)

| Plan | Price/mo | Users | Key Features for Trailhead |

### QBO-Only Strategy (No Additional Software)

If you use ONLY QuickBooks Online for everything:
- Set up recurring invoice templates for each subscription customer
- Enable QuickBooks Payments for card/ACH autopay
- Use the customer list as a basic "CRM"
- Track expenses and revenue natively
- Manually manage scheduling (Google Calendar, pen and paper, etc.)

**This can work for 25-50 customers but becomes painful at 50-100 without dedicated scheduling and route optimization software. The time spent on manual scheduling and routing at 50+ stops per week costs more than the software to automate it.**

## 6. RECOMMENDATIONS

### RECOMMENDED PATH: Phased Approach

---

#### PHASE 1: Starting Out (0-50 Customers)
**Use: LawnPro FREE plan + QuickBooks Online Simple Start**

- **Total monthly cost:** $38/mo (QBO only; LawnPro is free for up to 50 clients)
- **What you get:** Scheduling, CRM, invoicing, recurring billing, QBO sync, mobile app, client portal
- **Why:** Zero software risk. Free to try. QBO handles the accounting. LawnPro handles the field operations. If LawnPro feels too basic, you have not lost any money. The 50-client free tier is generous enough to validate the entire subscription model.

**Alternative starting point:** Yardbook (free) if you want chemical tracking and lot measurement, but know that QBO sync requires the $50/mo Enterprise plan. Or use QBO alone with recurring invoices + Google Calendar for scheduling if you want the absolute simplest setup.

---

#### PHASE 2: Growing (25-75 Customers, Feeling the Pain of Manual Processes)
**Use: Jobber Core ($69/mo) + QuickBooks Online Simple Start ($38/mo)**

- **Total monthly cost:** ~$107/mo
- **What you get:** Best-in-class scheduling with route optimization, client hub (customer portal), automated invoicing and follow-ups, mobile app for field work, QBO sync, online booking, AI-powered job pricing
- **Why:** Jobber is the industry standard for lawn care businesses at this scale. The route optimization alone can save 1-2 hours per day in drive time. The client hub lets customers self-serve (approve quotes, pay invoices, check appointments), reducing admin time significantly. The mobile app is genuinely excellent for solo operators in the field.
- **Transaction fees at 75 customers, $150/mo avg = $11,250/mo revenue:**
  - Card processing (2.9% + $0.30): ~$349/mo
  - If you push ACH instead (1%): ~$112/mo
  - Annual savings from ACH: ~$2,844

---

#### PHASE 3: Scaling (75-100+ Customers, Hiring Help)
**Use: Jobber Connect ($169/mo) + QuickBooks Online Essentials ($75/mo)**

- **Total monthly cost:** ~$244/mo
- **What you get:** Everything in Phase 2 plus up to 5 users (for crews/employees), time tracking for payroll, advanced reporting, automated quote follow-ups
- **Why:** When you add employees, you need multi-user access, time tracking, and better reporting. Jobber Connect and QBO Essentials both scale to this level. The $244/mo is easily justified when managing $15K+/mo in subscription revenue.

---

### ALTERNATIVE RECOMMENDATION: Budget Path (If $69/mo for Jobber feels steep)

**GorillaDesk Pro ($99/mo) + QBO Simple Start ($38/mo) = $137/mo**
- More lawn-care specific than Jobber
- Includes chemical tracking
- But costs $30/mo MORE than Jobber Core + QBO for equivalent QBO sync functionality
- Mobile app is less reliable

**Or: GorillaDesk Basic ($49/mo) + QBO Simple Start ($38/mo) = $87/mo**
- $20/mo cheaper than Jobber path
- But NO QuickBooks sync on Basic plan
- You would need to manually reconcile between GorillaDesk and QBO
- Not recommended if QBO integration is a requirement

**Bottom line: Jobber at $69/mo + $38/mo QBO = $107/mo is the best value when QBO sync is required.**

---

### WHAT TO AVOID

1. **ServiceTitan** -- Way too expensive ($3,000-6,000/year + $5,000+ setup) and complex. Designed for $5M+ businesses with 20+ techs.
2. **Standalone CRM (HubSpot, Zoho, Monday)** -- Unnecessary complexity. The CRM in Jobber or GorillaDesk is sufficient for 25-100 customers. Adding a separate CRM creates data silos and double-entry.
3. **Stripe alone** -- Great payment processor but requires building everything else around it. No native QBO sync ($50+/mo for third-party connector). Better as a behind-the-scenes processor (which Jobber and GorillaDesk already use under the hood).
4. **Zoho ecosystem** -- Powerful but pulls you away from QuickBooks and requires managing multiple Zoho products (Billing + CRM + Books). More complexity than a solo lawn care operator needs.
5. **Square alone** -- Good payment processing and native QBO sync, but no field service features. Better as a supplementary payment method, not a primary business platform.

### ACH / BANK TRANSFER STRATEGY (Important Cost Saver)

Regardless of which platform you choose, pushing customers toward ACH/bank transfer payments instead of credit cards is one of the highest-impact cost decisions:

| Payment Method | Fee on $150 Transaction | Annual Cost per Customer (Monthly Billing) | Annual Cost for 100 Customers |
|---------------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Credit Card (2.9% + $0.30) | $4.65 | $55.80 | $5,580 |
| ACH via Jobber (1%) | $1.50 | $18.00 | $1,800 |
| ACH via Stripe (0.8%, $5 cap) | $1.20 | $14.40 | $1,440 |

**Savings of $3,780-$4,140/year at 100 customers by using ACH instead of cards.**

To encourage ACH adoption:
- Offer a small discount ($5-10/mo off) for customers who pay via bank transfer
- Set ACH as the default payment method during onboarding
- Frame it as "autopay from your bank account" (sounds more trustworthy than "credit card on file")

### KEY TAKEAWAY

**Start with LawnPro (free, up to 50 clients) + QuickBooks Online Simple Start ($38/mo) to validate your subscription model at zero additional software cost. When you hit ~50 customers or feel the pain of limited scheduling and routing features, upgrade to Jobber Core ($69/mo). Push customers toward ACH/bank transfer payments to minimize processing fees. The all-in-one field service platforms (Jobber, GorillaDesk) are far better value than cobbling together Stripe + a CRM + a scheduling tool + a QBO connector.**
