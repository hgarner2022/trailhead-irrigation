### Key Observations

1. **Very few competitors publish pricing online** in the Erie/Longmont area specifically. This is an opportunity for Trailhead to differentiate with transparent pricing.

2. **Maintenance/subscription plans are rare locally**. Colorado Sprinkler Repair and Sunlight Sprinkler are the only ones with published tiered plans, and neither is a direct local competitor. This is a major whitespace opportunity.

3. **Pricing is remarkably consistent** across the market: $80-$99 for seasonal services (turn-on and winterization) up to 10 zones.

4. **No competitor in the immediate Erie/Longmont/Louisville/Lafayette market has a published subscription plan**. Pierce, JLB, Green Guys, Flatiron, Mr. Greenthumb, and Ward's all operate on a per-service basis with no visible recurring revenue model.

5. **Smart controller rebate programs** from Longmont, Lafayette, and Erie create a natural sales funnel - customers getting free controllers need someone to install and maintain them.
